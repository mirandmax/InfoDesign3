How income inequality and tax revenues have evolved across continents since 1985 and what the Gini coefficient tells us about where we are today.

By Diego Grünberger, Max Mirandola and Elias Puntaier

Inequality across continents

The Gini coefficient measures the distribution of income within a country, where 0 represents perfect equality and 1 perfect inequality. Aggregated by continent, the radar chart below reveals the structural differences in how wealth is spread — differences that have proven remarkably persistent over decades.

Africa consistently records the highest levels of income inequality, while Europe's social welfare systems have kept inequality comparatively low. Asia and the Americas occupy a broad middle ground, though with considerable variation between countries.

How to read this chart: Each axis represents a continent. Values further from the center indicate higher inequality (higher Gini). The shaded area shows the mean Gini for the most recent available year.

The Intuitive Assumption

High revenue equals high redistribution. It sounds logical—but it only holds up at first glance

If we want to fight inequality, the solution seems obvious: tax more, redistribute more. If this logic holds true, countries with the highest tax revenues should boast the lowest levels of inequality. At first glance, the data seems to back this up: Western and Northern Europe (high taxes) cluster tightly in the low-inequality corner. Parts of Africa and Latin America stand in stark contrast on the opposite side.

But appearances can be deceiving. Watch what happens when we scale the circles by economic power (GDP per capita) and color them by continent. Suddenly, the illusion fades. The data points scatter wildly. Countries with identical tax ratios exhibit vastly different Gini coefficients. The hidden driver behind this beautiful correlation isn't the tax rate itself—it's wealth. Rich nations possess deep-rooted, stable institutions; they can afford to collect high taxes because they already have a robust middle class.
